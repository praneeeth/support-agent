# Your account

- You can check out as a guest; an account is only needed for Northwind Circle points and order history.
- To reset your password, use Forgot Password on the sign-in page; the link is valid for 30 minutes.
- To change your email address or delete your account, contact our support team.
- Guest orders can still be tracked with the order number and checkout email.
