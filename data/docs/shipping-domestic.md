# Shipping within India

Northwind Goods ships all orders from our warehouse in Pune, Maharashtra.

## Standard shipping
Standard shipping is free on orders of ₹1,999 or more. Orders below ₹1,999 are charged a flat ₹99.
Delivery takes 3–5 business days to metro cities and 5–8 business days to other locations.

## Express shipping
Express shipping costs ₹249 and delivers in 1–2 business days. It is available only to these metro
cities: Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Chennai, Hyderabad, Pune and Kolkata.

## Dispatch times
Orders placed before 2:00 pm IST, Monday to Saturday, are dispatched the same day. Orders placed after
2:00 pm, or on Sundays and public holidays, are dispatched the next business day.

## Carriers
Domestic orders are delivered by Blue Dart or Delhivery. The carrier is chosen automatically based on
your PIN code and cannot be selected at checkout.
