# How to make a warranty claim

To make a warranty claim, email warranty@northwindgoods.example with your order number, a
description of the problem and clear photos. You can also ask the support assistant to connect you
to our team. We review claims within 5 business days and will repair, replace, or issue store
credit for the item. Proof of purchase (your order number) is required.
