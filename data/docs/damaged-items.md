# Damaged or defective items

## Report within 48 hours
If an item arrives damaged or defective, report it within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the
item and the packaging.

## What we do
We will send a free replacement, or give a full refund including shipping if you prefer.
For damaged items worth less than ₹1,000 you do not need to send the item back.

## Defects discovered later
Manufacturing defects found after 48 hours are covered by the product warranty — see the warranty pages.
