# Bedding size guide

Our bed sizes (mattress dimensions):
- Single: 90 × 190 cm
- Double: 135 × 190 cm
- Queen: 150 × 200 cm
- King: 180 × 200 cm

Fitted sheets fit mattresses up to 30 cm deep. Duvet covers are sized 10 cm larger than the
matching duvet insert. Pillowcases are 50 × 75 cm (standard).
