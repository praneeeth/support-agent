# Refunds

## When refunds are issued
Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days after your return is received and inspected.

## Where the money goes
Refunds go back to the original payment method. UPI and card refunds usually show in your account
within 5–7 business days after we process them, depending on your bank.

## Cash on delivery orders
Cash on delivery orders are refunded by NEFT bank transfer (we will ask for your bank details) or as
store credit.

## Store credit bonus
If you choose store credit instead of a refund to your original payment method, you get an extra 5%
added to the credit. Store credit is valid for 12 months.

## Shipping charges
Original shipping charges are refunded only if the item was damaged, defective or wrong.
