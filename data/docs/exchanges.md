# Exchanges

We offer free exchanges for a different size or colour of the same product within 30 days of
delivery, subject to stock. Each order item can be exchanged once. If the replacement is out of
stock, we will offer a refund or store credit instead. Exchanges are arranged by our support team
and use the same free pickup as returns within India.
