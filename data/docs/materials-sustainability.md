# Materials and sustainability

- Our cotton bedding and towels are OEKO-TEX Standard 100 certified.
- Wooden products use FSC-certified wood.
- Linen is made from European flax, woven in India.
- All orders ship in plastic-free packaging made from recycled cardboard and paper tape.
