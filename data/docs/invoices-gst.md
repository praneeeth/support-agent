# Invoices and GST

A GST invoice is emailed automatically when your order is dispatched. To claim input tax credit,
add your company name and GSTIN at checkout. A GSTIN cannot be added to an order after it has been
dispatched. For a copy of an invoice, ask our support assistant or email support@northwindgoods.example.
