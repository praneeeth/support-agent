# Payment methods

## Accepted in India
- UPI (any UPI app)
- Credit and debit cards: Visa, Mastercard, RuPay and American Express
- Net banking
- Wallets: Paytm and PhonePe
- No-cost EMI on credit cards for orders over ₹3,000 (3 or 6 months)
- Cash on delivery (COD) for orders up to ₹10,000, with a ₹49 COD fee

## International orders
International credit/debit cards and PayPal only.

## Security
We never ask for your full card number, CVV, OTP or UPI PIN over chat, email or phone.
