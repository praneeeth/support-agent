# International shipping

We ship internationally to the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, United Arab Emirates
and Singapore. We do not ship to other countries yet.

## Cost and delivery time
International shipping is a flat ₹2,499 per order, and free on orders of ₹15,000 or more.
Delivery takes 7–12 business days. International orders are shipped with DHL Express or FedEx.

## Customs and duties
Import duties and taxes are charged by the destination country and are paid by the customer on
delivery. Northwind Goods cannot predict or refund these charges. If you refuse to pay duties, the
parcel is returned to us and the refund excludes the original shipping charge.

## Payment for international orders
International orders can be paid only by international credit/debit card or PayPal. Cash on
delivery is not available outside India.
