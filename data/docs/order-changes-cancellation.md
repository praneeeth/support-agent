# Changing or cancelling an order

## Changing an order
You can change the delivery address, items or quantities within 2 hours of placing the order, as long
as it is still in Processing status. Changes are made by our support team — ask the assistant to
connect you to a person.

## Cancelling an order
Orders can be cancelled free of charge while they are in Processing status. Once an order has been
shipped it cannot be cancelled; instead, accept delivery and start a return within 30 days.
Cancellations are handled by our support team, not by the automated assistant.

## Refund for cancelled orders
Prepaid orders cancelled before dispatch are refunded in full within 5–7 business days.
