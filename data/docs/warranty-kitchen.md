# Kitchen warranty

- Cast iron skillets and enamel Dutch ovens: lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects.
- Chef's knives: 5-year warranty against manufacturing defects.
- Wooden and bamboo cutting boards: 1-year warranty.
- Other kitchen items: 1-year warranty.

The warranty does not cover damage from misuse, such as putting cast iron or wooden items in the
dishwasher, overheating an empty enamel pot, or using a knife to cut bone or frozen food.
