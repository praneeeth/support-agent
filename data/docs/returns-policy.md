# Returns policy

## Return window
You can return most items within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused, unwashed, and in the
original packaging with tags attached.

## Items that cannot be returned
- Items marked Final Sale
- Personalised or monogrammed items
- Pillows and duvet inserts once the sealed packaging has been opened (for hygiene reasons)
- Gift cards

## How to return
Returns are arranged by our support team. For orders within India we schedule a free return pickup
from your delivery address. For international orders, return shipping is paid by the customer and
the parcel must be sent with a tracked service.

## After we receive your return
Returned items are inspected within 2 business days of arriving at our warehouse. See the Refunds
page for how and when you are refunded.
