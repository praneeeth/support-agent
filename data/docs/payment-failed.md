# Payment failed or money debited without an order

If your payment failed but money was debited from your account, the amount is automatically
reversed by your bank within 5–7 business days. You do not need to contact us. If it has not been
reversed after 7 business days, contact support with the transaction reference and we will help
trace it with our payment provider. Please place the order again if you still want the items.
