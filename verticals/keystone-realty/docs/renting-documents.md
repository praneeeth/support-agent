# Documents for renting

Tenants need:
- Photo ID (Aadhaar, passport or driving licence) and PAN card for every adult.
- Proof of employment: an offer letter or employee ID.
- The last two salary slips, or the last two years' ITR if self-employed.
- Two passport-size photos.
