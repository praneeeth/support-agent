# How viewings work

- Tell us what you're looking for, your budget and how to reach you. An agent calls you within
  one working day with homes that match.
- Viewings are arranged by the agent, usually within 2 days, between 10 am and 7 pm.
- Viewings are free. An agent always comes with you.
- Listings change daily. Only an agent can confirm that a home is still available.
