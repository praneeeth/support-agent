# Our fees

| Service | Fee |
|---|---|
| Renting (paid by the tenant) | one month's rent, plus GST |
| Renting (paid by the owner) | one month's rent, plus GST |
| Buying | 1% of the sale price, plus GST |
| Selling | 1% of the sale price, plus GST |
| Property management | 8% of the monthly rent |
| Rental agreement registration | ₹1,500 service fee plus government charges |

Fees are due when the agreement is signed. There is nothing to pay for viewings.
