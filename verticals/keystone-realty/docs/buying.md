# Buying a home

- We show resale and ready-to-move homes. We don't sell under-construction flats for developers.
- We check that the seller holds a clear title and arrange a lawyer's title search for ₹7,500.
- Stamp duty and registration are paid by the buyer; the lawyer confirms the amount.
