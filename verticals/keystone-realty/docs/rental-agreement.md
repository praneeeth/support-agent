# Rental agreements

- Homes in Maharashtra are rented on a registered leave and licence agreement, usually for 11
  months.
- We draft the agreement and arrange online registration: ₹1,500 service fee plus stamp duty and
  registration charges, usually shared equally by owner and tenant.
- Police verification of tenants is done online as part of registration.
