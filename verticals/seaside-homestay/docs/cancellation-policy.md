# Cancellation policy

- Cancel 14 or more days before arrival: the full advance is refunded.
- Cancel 7 to 13 days before arrival: half of the advance is refunded.
- Cancel less than 7 days before arrival, or don't arrive: the advance is not refunded.
- Refunds go back to the account the advance came from within 7 working days.
- If we have to cancel (for example after a cyclone warning), you get a full refund.

To cancel, contact the owner. The assistant can't cancel bookings or issue refunds.
