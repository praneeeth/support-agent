# Meals

## Breakfast
- Included with every room, served 8:00–10:00 am on the terrace.
- South Indian (idli, dosa, poha) and continental (eggs, toast, fruit) are both available.

## Lunch and dinner
- Home-cooked Konkani thali on request with 4 hours' notice: ₹450 vegetarian, ₹600 with fish.
- Tell us about allergies or dietary needs at least 24 hours ahead; we can cook vegan and
  gluten-free.
- Guests in the Family Cottage can also cook in its kitchenette.
