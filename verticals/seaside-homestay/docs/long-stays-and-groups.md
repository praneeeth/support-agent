# Long stays and groups

## Long stays
- Stays of 7 nights or more get 10% off the room rate. The owner applies it when she confirms.
- The longest stay we take is 14 nights.

## Groups
- The whole house (all three rooms, up to 9 guests) can be booked together, subject to the
  owner's confirmation.
- We don't host weddings, parties or events.
