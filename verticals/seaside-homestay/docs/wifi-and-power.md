# Wi-Fi and power

- Free fibre Wi-Fi throughout the house, usually around 50 Mbps.
- Power cuts happen in Gokarna. An inverter keeps lights, fans and Wi-Fi running; air
  conditioning does not run on the inverter.
- Hot water comes from solar heaters with an electric geyser as backup.
