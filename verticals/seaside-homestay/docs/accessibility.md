# Accessibility

- The Garden Room is on the ground floor with step-free access from the gate. Its bathroom
  has a grab rail but no roll-in shower.
- The Sea View Room is on the first floor and is reached by stairs only.
- The Family Cottage has three steps up to its veranda.
- The lane to the house is unpaved for the last 200 m.
