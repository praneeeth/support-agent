# Changing your dates

- You can move your dates once, free of charge, up to 14 days before arrival, if the new
  dates are free.
- Changes made less than 14 days before arrival are treated as a cancellation.
- Adding a night to an existing booking depends on availability and is confirmed by the owner.

Only the owner can change a booking.
