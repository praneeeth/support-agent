# Our rooms

Seaside Homestay has three rooms in one family home, a 10-minute walk from Kudle Beach in Gokarna.

| Room | Sleeps | Beds | Air conditioning | Rate per night |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sea View Room | 2 | one queen bed | yes | ₹4,500 |
| Garden Room | 3 | one double bed and one single bed | no, ceiling fan | ₹3,800 |
| Family Cottage | 4 | two bedrooms, each with a double bed | yes | ₹6,500 |

- Every room has its own attached bathroom with hot water.
- Rates include breakfast for everyone staying in the room and all taxes.
- The whole house sleeps 9 guests when all three rooms are taken together.
