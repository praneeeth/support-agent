# Rates and what's included

## Published rates (per room, per night)
- Sea View Room: ₹4,500
- Garden Room: ₹3,800
- Family Cottage: ₹6,500

## Included
- Breakfast for every registered guest in the room, served 8:00–10:00 am.
- All taxes (GST is included in the rates above).
- Wi-Fi, filtered drinking water, beach towels, parking and the morning yoga session.

## Not included
- Lunch and dinner, airport or station transfers, laundry, surfing lessons and late check-out.

Rates are fixed and the same on every booking channel. We don't negotiate rates. The only
discount is the long-stay discount described under Long stays and groups.
