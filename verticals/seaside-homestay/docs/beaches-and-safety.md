# Beach safety

- Lifeguards are on duty at Kudle and Om beaches from October to May, 9:00 am to 6:00 pm.
- Never swim after dark or when the red flag is up.
- Swimming is not allowed from June to September; the sea is rough in the monsoon.
- We keep a first-aid kit at reception.
- The nearest hospital is Gokarna Primary Health Centre (3 km). The nearest larger hospital is
  in Kumta (25 km).
