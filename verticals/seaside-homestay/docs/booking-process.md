# How booking works

We don't take instant bookings. Every stay is confirmed personally by Meera, the owner.

1. Send an enquiry with your dates, number of guests, your name and an email or phone number.
   You can do this here in chat, on WhatsApp or by email.
2. Meera checks the calendar and replies, usually within 4 hours between 8am and 8pm.
3. If the dates are free, she sends a payment link for the 30% advance.
4. Your booking is confirmed only when the advance is paid. You then get a booking reference
   in the format SS-1234.

Nothing is held or reserved until the advance is paid. The minimum stay is 2 nights and the
longest stay we take is 14 nights.
