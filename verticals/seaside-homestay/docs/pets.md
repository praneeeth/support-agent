# Pets

- Dogs are welcome in the Garden Room only. It opens onto the garden.
- One dog per booking, at ₹500 per night.
- Dogs must be on a leash in shared areas and are not allowed on the beds.
- Please mention your dog when you send your enquiry.
- We can't take cats or other pets.
