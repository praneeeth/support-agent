# Children and extra beds

- Children of all ages are welcome.
- Children under 5 stay free when they share their parents' bed.
- One extra mattress for a child aged 5 to 11 costs ₹800 per night, including breakfast. It is
  available in the Garden Room and the Family Cottage only, on top of the room's usual maximum.
- Guests aged 12 and over count as adults.
- A cot is available free on request.
