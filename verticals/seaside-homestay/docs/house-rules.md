# House rules

- Quiet hours are 10:00 pm to 7:00 am.
- No visitors in the rooms after 8:00 pm.
- No smoking indoors. Smoking is allowed in the garden and on balconies.
- Parties and events are not allowed.
- Alcohol may be enjoyed quietly in your room, not in shared areas.
- Please switch off the air conditioning when you leave the room.
