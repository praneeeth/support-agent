# Things to do

- Free yoga on the terrace every morning at 7:00 am, October to May.
- Surfing lessons with our partner school: ₹2,500 per 90-minute session, booked through us.
- The beach trek from Kudle to Om, Half Moon and Paradise beaches takes about 3 hours one way.
- Kayak hire at Om Beach costs about ₹500 an hour, paid directly to the operator.
- Mahabaleshwar Temple in Gokarna town is 3 km away.
