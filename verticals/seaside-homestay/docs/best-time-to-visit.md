# Best time to visit

- October to March: dry and sunny, 22–32°C. The busiest season.
- April and May: hot and humid, up to 35°C.
- June to September: monsoon. Heavy rain, rough sea, no swimming. The homestay stays open.
- 20 December to 5 January is usually fully booked months ahead.
