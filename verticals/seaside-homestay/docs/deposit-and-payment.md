# Advance and payment

- A 30% advance confirms a booking. The payment link is sent by the owner after she confirms
  your dates.
- The advance can be paid by UPI or bank transfer.
- The remaining 70% is paid at check-in by UPI, cash, or debit/credit card. There is no card
  surcharge.
- We never ask for card details, OTPs or UPI PINs over chat, WhatsApp or email.
