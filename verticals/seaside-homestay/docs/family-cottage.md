# Family Cottage

- A separate cottage in the garden with two bedrooms, each with a double bed. Sleeps up to 4.
- One shared bathroom, a small sitting area and a kitchenette with a fridge, kettle and
  two-burner stove.
- Air conditioning in both bedrooms.
- ₹6,500 per night, including breakfast for four and taxes.
- Three steps lead up to the cottage veranda.
- One extra mattress can be added for a child aged 5 to 11 (see Children and extra beds).
