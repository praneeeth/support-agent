# Getting here

- Address: Seaside Homestay, Kudle Beach Road, Gokarna, Uttara Kannada, Karnataka 581326.
- Kudle Beach is a 10-minute walk. Om Beach is 2.5 km away and Gokarna town is 3 km.
- Nearest railway stations: Gokarna Road (9 km) and Kumta (25 km).
- Nearest airports: Goa (Dabolim), about 150 km or 3.5 hours by road, and Hubballi, about 170 km.
- The last 200 m is a narrow lane; large buses can't reach the gate.
