# Check-in and check-out

- Check-in is from 1:00 pm to 9:00 pm.
- Check-out is by 11:00 am.
- Arriving after 9:00 pm? Tell us in advance and we'll leave the key with the caretaker.
- Early check-in is free if the room is ready; we can't guarantee it.
- Late check-out until 2:00 pm costs ₹1,000 and depends on the next booking.
- Luggage can be left with us free of charge until 6:00 pm on the day you leave.
