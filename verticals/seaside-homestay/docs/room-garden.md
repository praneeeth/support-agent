# Garden Room

- Sleeps up to 3 guests: one double bed and one single bed.
- Ground-floor room opening onto the garden, with step-free access from the gate.
- Ceiling fan and mosquito net. The Garden Room has no air conditioning.
- ₹3,800 per night, including breakfast and taxes.
- The Garden Room is the only room where dogs are allowed.
- One extra mattress can be added for a child aged 5 to 11 (see Children and extra beds).
