# Caring for towels and bath textiles

- Wash before first use to remove finishing residue.
- Machine wash at up to 60°C. Avoid fabric softener, which reduces absorbency.
- Tumble dry on medium or line dry.
- Turkish towels are thinner and dry faster than standard terry towels.
