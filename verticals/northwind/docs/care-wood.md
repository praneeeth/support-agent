# Caring for wooden products

Applies to bamboo cutting boards, teak bath mats and acacia serving trays.
- Hand wash with warm soapy water and dry immediately. Never use the dishwasher.
- Oil once a month with food-safe mineral oil (cutting boards and trays) or teak oil (bath mats).
- Do not leave wooden products standing in water.
