# Price changes and price matching

We do not price match other retailers. If the price of an item you bought drops on our website
within 7 days of your purchase, we give a one-time price adjustment for the difference as store
credit. Price adjustments do not apply to flash sales or discount-code savings. Contact our support
team with your order number to request one.
