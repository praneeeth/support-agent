# Bedding and bath warranty

Bedding and bath products (sheets, duvet covers, quilts, throws, towels, bathrobes) have a 1-year
warranty against manufacturing defects such as split seams and failed stitching.
Normal wear, pilling, shrinkage from washing above the recommended temperature, and colour fading
from bleach are not covered.
