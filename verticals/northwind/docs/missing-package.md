# Package marked delivered but not received

If tracking shows Delivered but you have not received your parcel:
1. Wait 24 hours — carriers sometimes mark parcels delivered early.
2. Check with neighbours, your building security or reception.
3. Contact us within 7 days of the delivery date.

We will open an investigation with the carrier, which takes 3–5 business days. If the parcel cannot
be found, we will send a replacement or issue a full refund.
