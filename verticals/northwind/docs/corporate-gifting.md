# Corporate and bulk orders

For orders of 20 or more units, we offer corporate pricing of 10% off (20–99 units) or 20% off
(100+ units), plus optional custom gift notes. Email corporate@northwindgoods.example with the
products, quantities and delivery date. Bulk orders need at least 10 business days' notice.
