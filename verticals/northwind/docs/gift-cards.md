# Gift cards

- Digital gift cards are available from ₹500 to ₹25,000 and are delivered by email.
- Gift cards are valid for 12 months from the date of purchase.
- Gift cards cannot be used to buy other gift cards.
- Gift cards are non-refundable and cannot be exchanged for cash.
- A gift card can be combined with one other payment method on the same order.
- Lost gift card codes can be re-sent to the original purchaser's email by our support team.
