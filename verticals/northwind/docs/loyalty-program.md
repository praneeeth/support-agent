# Northwind Circle loyalty programme

Northwind Circle is free to join for anyone with an account.
- Earn 1 point for every ₹100 spent. Points are added when the order is delivered.
- 100 points can be redeemed for ₹100 off a future order.
- Points expire 12 months after they are earned.
- Points are removed if the order they were earned on is returned.
