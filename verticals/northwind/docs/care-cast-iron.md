# Caring for cast iron

- Hand wash only with hot water and a brush; a little mild soap is fine.
- Never put cast iron in the dishwasher or leave it soaking.
- Dry immediately on the stove over low heat, then rub a thin layer of oil on the surface.
- If rust appears, scrub it off with steel wool, rinse, dry and re-season in the oven at 230°C for one hour.
- Our cast iron comes pre-seasoned with flaxseed oil.
