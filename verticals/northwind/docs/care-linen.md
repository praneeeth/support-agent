# Caring for linen

- Machine wash at up to 40°C on a gentle cycle with mild detergent.
- Do not use bleach.
- Tumble dry on low or line dry. Remove while slightly damp to reduce creasing.
- Linen softens with every wash. Expect up to 3% shrinkage on the first wash.
