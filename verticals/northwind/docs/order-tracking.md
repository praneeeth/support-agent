# Tracking your order

## How to track
When your order ships, we send an email and SMS with the carrier name and tracking number. You can
also ask our support assistant for your order status using your order number (it starts with NW-)
and the email address used at checkout.

## What each order status means
- **Processing** — we have received your order and are packing it.
- **Shipped** — your parcel has left our warehouse; tracking is active.
- **Delivered** — the carrier has marked the parcel as delivered.
- **Cancelled** — the order was cancelled before dispatch; any payment is refunded.
- **Return requested** — we have received your return request and a pickup is being arranged.
- **Refunded** — your refund has been issued to the original payment method or as store credit.

## Tracking not updating
Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan after dispatch. If there is no update for
more than 3 business days, contact support and we will raise it with the carrier.
