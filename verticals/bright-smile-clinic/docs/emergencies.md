# Dental emergencies

- During opening hours, call or WhatsApp us and come in: emergencies are seen the same day.
- Outside opening hours, go to the emergency department of Sahyadri Hospital, Kothrud (2 km),
  which is open 24 hours.
- Go to an emergency department straight away for swelling that affects breathing or
  swallowing, heavy bleeding that won't stop, or a knocked-out adult tooth.
