# About Bright Smile Dental

Bright Smile Dental is a family dental clinic in Kothrud, Pune, run by Dr. Neha Kulkarni.

## What we do
- Check-ups and cleaning
- Fillings, root canal treatment and crowns
- Tooth extraction, including wisdom teeth
- Teeth whitening
- Braces and clear aligners (consultation first)
- Children's dentistry from age 3

We don't offer dental implants or treatment under general anaesthesia; we refer these to
partner hospitals.
