# After a tooth extraction

These are the notes the dentist gives you after an extraction.

- Some bleeding for up to 24 hours is normal. Bite on the gauze for 30 minutes.
- Don't rinse, spit or drink through a straw for 24 hours.
- Swelling usually peaks on the second day.
- Take pain relief only as your dentist prescribed.
