# Location and parking

- Address: 2nd floor, Vishwa Plaza, Karve Road, Kothrud, Pune 411038.
- There is a lift in the building.
- Two-wheeler parking is free in the basement. There is no car parking; use the paid lot
  opposite the building.
- Nearest metro: Vanaz station, a 5-minute walk.
