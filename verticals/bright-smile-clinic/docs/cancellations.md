# Cancelling or moving an appointment

- Please give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or move an appointment.
- A missed appointment, or one cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice, carries a ₹500 fee,
  added to your next bill.
- Running late? We hold your slot for 15 minutes.
