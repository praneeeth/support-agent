# Payment and insurance

- We accept UPI, debit and credit cards and cash. There is no card surcharge.
- Payment is due at the end of each visit.
- Treatments over ₹20,000 can be paid in up to three instalments, agreed in advance.
- We don't offer cashless insurance. We give an itemised receipt and treatment notes for you to
  claim from your insurer.
