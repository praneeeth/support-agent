# How appointments work

- We see patients by appointment only, except for dental emergencies during opening hours.
- To ask for an appointment, give your name, a phone number or email and a preferred day and
  time. Our front desk calls you back within one working day to agree a slot.
- An appointment is confirmed only when the front desk has called or messaged you with the time.
- A check-up or cleaning takes about 45 minutes. A first consultation takes about 30 minutes.
